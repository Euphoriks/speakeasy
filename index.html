Questions

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. After the one-time speech engine download, dictation runs entirely on your phone. Turn on airplane mode and try it.

Is my voice recorded or uploaded?

No. Audio is processed on your phone and never sent anywhere. Your recent dictations are kept only on your phone, and you can turn that off.

Why does it ask for Accessibility?

Android only lets an app show the mic bubble above your keyboard and type into other apps through its Accessibility service. SpeakEasy doesn't read your screen or messages. Prefer not to? Use the SpeakEasy Keyboard instead.

Which phones does it work on?

Android 10 or newer. Faster phones get a more accurate speech engine and live preview; older phones get a lighter one.

Which languages?

English for now, with Canadian, British or American spelling.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. SpeakEasy is Android only for now.
